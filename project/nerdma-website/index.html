Nerdma Systems — a website redesign that finally explains the product.

The brief

Strong product suite. Weak front door.

Nerdma Systems builds three enterprise ICT products. The old site was a generic brochure — it listed services, not products. It ranked for nothing. Every enquiry landed in the inbox without context.

The MD: "Rare to find someone who can design a site this sharp and then think through the sales pipeline behind it." That was the brief — sharp design, smart pipeline, all in one pass.

Discovery

What the audit found.

Before touching Figma, a full content and competitive audit was done against ten SA ICT services firms.

Generic copy

"Managed IT services for business" described ten thousand other companies. Zero product differentiation. NerdGrid, NerdGrow and NerdScore were invisible on the homepage.

Zero SEO foundation

No structured content, no keyword targeting, no local search presence. The site ranked only for the brand name — nothing a new customer would search.

Dead-end enquiry flow

A contact form with no context field — delivering name and phone number only. No product interest, no company size, no urgency captured.

No follow-up system

Leads went into a shared inbox with no ownership, no timeline, no follow-up. Revenue sat unworked.

How it was done

A site that had to win technical buyers, not just browsers.

Same four moves as any project — but here every one of them was aimed at a buyer who reads the fine print.

01 · Discovery

Started from the deals Nerdma loses: the first technical conversation, where it stalls, what proof the other side asks for. The old site was torn down page by page, ten SA ICT rivals benchmarked, and the three product lines — NerdGrid, NerdGrow, NerdScore — got their own keyword maps. The brief was signed before a single pixel was drawn.

02 · Structure

The sitemap was rebuilt around the products, not the departments that own them. Each product gets a home, use cases carry the proof, and contact sits one click away from anywhere. The team signed off wireframes before any colour was applied.

04 · Build

Designed in Figma, shipped as semantic HTML with structured data on every product page. The HubSpot form carries product interest, company size and urgency — so an enquiry lands as a deal, not a question.

05 · Launch

QA on the devices the buyers actually use, GA4 and Search Console counting before the first click, and a one-page edit guide so the MD's office keeps the site current between deals.

The solution

Product-led, pipeline-backed.

Product homepage

Each product gets a hero row above the fold. Visitors know within three seconds what Nerdma builds and why it matters.

Dedicated product pages

NerdGrid, NerdGrow and NerdScore each have their own page: use cases, features, differentiators, direct CTA.

SEO content

Pillar pages targeting ICT decision-maker queries. Blog and resource section for authority building over time.

Smart enquiry form

Multi-step form: product interest, company size, urgency, budget range. Context the sales team actually needs.

CRM pipeline

HubSpot sequences triggered on submission. Automated, personal, on-brand follow-up for every product line.

Analytics baseline

GA4 + Search Console from day one. Organic traffic, conversions and pipeline value tracked monthly.
