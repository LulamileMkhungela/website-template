The brief

Portfolio-rich. Web-presence-poor.

AddmoreDigital had delivered strong work across branding, social and web for several South African businesses. The problem: their own site didn't show any of it. A generic agency template, no portfolio structure, and a contact form that delivered name-and-email with zero context.

The brief: restructure around the portfolio, get the site ranking, and build a pipeline so enquiries arrive ready for a proposal conversation — not a discovery call from scratch every time.

The challenge

Every agency looks the same online.

  • Generic service list: "branding, social media, web design" — the same words on five hundred agency sites.
  • Invisible portfolio: work existed but was buried in a disorganised gallery with no context or results.
  • No discovery SEO: the agency ranked for its own name only. Nothing for the service keywords its clients search.
  • Manual proposal process: every enquiry required an in-person discovery call before a brief could be written.
  • No follow-up: leads went cold in a shared inbox with no ownership or timeline.

How we got there

Work first, words second, pipeline underneath.

01 · Discovery

One interview, zero assumptions. Every page of the old site was audited, the agency's own body of work pulled back into the open, and shortlisted to the ten pieces worth framing properly. The enquiry path — search, service, brief — was mapped on paper before layout started.

02 · Structure

Service-first, always: each service got its own page, the portfolio sat underneath, and every project page answers brief, execution and result. The company story moved to about — the navigation now does the selling.

04 · Build

Portfolio cards bold enough to carry the work, copy written to rank, everything semantic and SEO-tight. HubSpot sat underneath each service line — pipeline, segmentation and follow-up sequences ready before launch.

05 · Launch

Tested end to end: form, CRM, follow-up email — mobile first. GSC and GA4 live from day one, and from that day the team updates its own portfolio without touching code.
